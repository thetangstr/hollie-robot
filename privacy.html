Hollie privacy policy

Hollie is a private household robot assistant used only by the family that owns it.

What Hollie accesses

With the family's permission, Hollie reads the family's Google Calendar events read-only (Google scopes calendar.events.readonly and calendar.calendarlist.readonly). Hollie never creates, edits or deletes calendar data. It does not access email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Calendar events are read only when someone at home asks Hollie a calendar question. They are used solely to answer that question aloud. Calendar data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It is not stored beyond what is needed to answer the question.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
